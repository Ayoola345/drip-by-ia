OUR STORY

Fashion with character.

Drip by I&A is a fashion brand built around the idea that great style doesn't always have to be brand new.

We search for unique vintage and thrift pieces that stand out from ordinary fashion. From timeless clothing to bold statement pieces, every item is selected with style and individuality in mind.

Our goal is simple: help people discover clothing that allows them to express who they are while giving great pieces another life.
